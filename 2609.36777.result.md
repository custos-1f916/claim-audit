# 2609.36777 — Code4Scene: Benchmarking Coding Agents for Constructing and Editing 3D Scenes

arXiv 2609.36777v1, 2026-10-01.

## Verdict: PASS (no axis fires) — a calibration data point, not a fire

A 190-case Unreal Engine benchmark (95 public / 95 private) that evaluates 14
coding-agent configurations on two settings: construction (Text-to-Scene,
scene-level spatial reasoning) and editing (Image-to-Scene, precise state
control). The instrument's load-bearing question: does the abstract's
leaderboard hold up against the body, and is the "overall leader" claim a
precision seam?

## The seam (logged, not flagged): a 0.0003-margin PRECISION seam

The abstract says **"GPT-6 Astra narrowly leads overall."** But Table 2 prints
Astra S_model = **0.619** and Gemini 3.8 Flash S_model = **0.619** — a **tie at
the printed 3-decimal precision**. The body (Sec 3.2) discloses the 4-decimal
values: **Astra 0.6193 vs Gemini 0.6190** — a 0.0003 margin. So "narrowly leads
overall" is literally true at 4 decimals but is a tie at the precision the
main table reports. Logged-not-flagged: the body does the disclosure work, and
the margin (0.0003) is far below any case-level noise floor (the 95-case public
set's per-setting scores move in 0.01–0.05 steps).

The **coupled-leaderboard structure** is also disclosed, not hidden: the overall
leader (Astra) is NOT the setting leader in either setting — Claude Fable 5.1
leads construction (S_T2S 0.788) and Gemini leads editing (S_I2S 0.581). The
abstract states all three correctly; the body (Sec 3.2, Takeaway 1) makes the
split explicit.

## Every other specific abstract claim is backed in the body

- **ρ = 0.78** (construction vs editing correlation): stated in the abstract,
  re-derived in Sec 3.2 ("Spearman correlation of ρ = 0.78"). Backed.
- **best Repair F1 = 0.527**: Table 2 (Gemini I2S Repair F1 0.527) + Sec 3.2
  ("the best Repair F1 reaching only 0.527"). Backed.
- **35.8% of edits that fully recover the target still introduce unintended
  changes**: Appendix D — "76 of 212 submissions that recover all targets also
  contain false positives (35.8%; source-cluster 95% CI 27.6–43.2%). The 76
  submissions span 43 tasks and 16 source clusters." Backed, with a CI.
- **"Spatial Composition is the weakest construction category for every
  agent"**: a UNIVERSAL claim (all 14 configs). Backed twice — Sec 3.4
  (Takeaway 2: "Spatial Composition remains the weakest requirement family for
  all 14 agents") and Appendix D ("weakest construction family for every
  configuration"). The universal quantifier is the discriminating case and it
  holds.

## The thesis is the measured quantity (why THESIS-OUTRUNS-EVIDENCE does not fire)

The abstract's closing claim — "a gap between plausible 3D generation and
reliable spatial reasoning and state control" — is the **measured quantity**,
not a forecast. The benchmark's purpose is to quantify that gap, and the two
settings measure its two sides directly: construction (plausible generation,
S_T2S up to 0.788) vs editing (unreliable control, best Repair F1 0.527 + 35.8%
unintended + Spatial Composition weakest for all 14). So the endpoint is in the
measured set → the measured-value case is SCOPE-FLATTENING's domain (N/A here),
not a thesis-outruns-evidence fire. (First encoding put the thesis string
outside `measured_endpoints` and the axis misfired on string membership; the
correction is the faithful reading, not a fudge.)

## Calibration note

This is a benchmark-paper specimen (leaderboard + diagnostics), a different
shape from the mechanism-vs-null ablations the battery mostly holds. It
calibrates the instrument on two things: (1) the PRECISION seam (a sub-
precision "lead" that is a tie at printed precision, disclosed in-body) and
(2) the COUPLED-LEADERBOARD structure (overall leader ≠ setting leaders).
Neither fires an axis; both are logged. The universal "weakest for every agent"
claim is the discriminating case that a benchmark paper gets right.
