**Authors:** Leonardo Ferreira, Gardenia Liu, Kaden Zheng
**Subjects:** cs.AI, cs.MA
**Verdict: SILENT / clean negative result** — a well-powered rejection of the
cognitive-diversity hypothesis, with a load-bearing measurement correction

finding of this audit, not a flaw in the paper.

## Load-bearing claim
"Reported MAD gains are an ensemble-sampling effect": multi-agent debate
does NOT beat budget-matched self-consistency sampling; the gains over
single-agent come from the sampling budget, not from deliberation or
cognitive diversity.

## Verified against the body (not the abstract)
- **Table 1 (context-corrected, 23-model pool, mean):** solo 80.4, debate
  84.4, SC-9 84.8, persona 81.9. Debate beats solo on headroom tasks
  (+3.3 GSM8K, +6.6 MMLU, +5.7 CSQA, p<0.002 Wilcoxon over models) but
  ties/loses to SC-9 at matched budget. Arithmetic at ceiling, unaffected.
- **Persona dose-response (5.2):** full combinatorial re-enumeration per
  model; redundant personas hurt most, maximally-diverse teams recover part
  of the loss -> "persona tax, not diversity tax." Significant on GSM8K
  n=3,5 and CSQA every size (−2.1 to −4.4pp, p≤0.005).
- **Mixed-model teams (5.3):** accuracy tracks member capability, not
  heterogeneity (SSS 93.7 -> MMM 87.0 -> WWW 75.6, monotone in tier
  composition). Matched ensembles reproduce and exceed the rescue effect
  (+16.4pp vs debate +13.5 in the 19 rescue cells).
- **Rounds ablation (5.1, Table 13):** essentially all deliberation benefit
  from the first exchange (84.6 at 1 round, 87.2 at 2, flat through 5).
- **Context-window overflow (5.5):** the measurement hazard — debate
  transcripts silently overflow serving context windows; the correction
  alone moves debate-vs-sampling from −1.8pp to parity. Disclosed and
  applied in Table 1 (the "context-corrected" policy).
- **Biography judged task (5.4):** SC-9 64.6% vs debate 55.6% claim
  precision (+8.9pp on unrounded means); judge vendor, not scale, dominates
  evaluation variance.

## The instrument finding (CLAIM-POLARITY boundary)
Framing A (rejected hypothesis = debate as `mechanism`, sampling as null)
fires **NULL-REACHES-HEADLINE as a FALSE POSITIVE**: the data (84.4 < 84.8)
SUPPORTS the paper's negative claim, but BEATS-NULL is claim-polarity-blind
and reads a supported negative claim as a flaw. Reframing with the winning
component as `mechanism` clears. This is the claim-level analog of the
metric-polarity witness (logged-not-flagged by design in check_beats_null):
metric polarity is a scale direction; claim polarity is a FRAMING boundary
where the wrong framing produces a false positive.
