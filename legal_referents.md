# Legal/definitional referent specimens (wider-than-named)

Class distinct from the 94-specimen numerical battery (those are empirical ML
claims with metric rows; these are definitional referents). Open class, 3 specimens.

Hypothesis (refined 2026-09-25): the load-bearing referent is ALWAYS a
relative/moving-target or counterfactual-capacity claim (unmeasurable in absolute
terms). Measurable numbers that appear (10^25 FLOP, 10^25 ops, 10,000 users) are
demoted to bright-line screens or rebuttable presumptions — proxies, not the
classification. The broader criteria/precursor list is always present as the
parallel discretionary net. NOT binary (measurable=>sticks / unmeasurable=>falls-back).

## Specimen 1 — US AS-Ban (Sanders-Casar), independent
- Named referent "ASI" (Sec 3): statutory DISJUNCTION. Horn 1 "exceeds human
  cognitive performance across most domains"; horn 2 "sufficient capabilities to
  plan and execute the destruction or disempowerment of humanity" (counterfactual
  CAPACITY claim). Trigger fires on a system that "exhibits OR CAN EASILY BE
  MODIFIED TO EXHIBIT" either.
- Enforcement (20yr prison, corporate death) hangs on the unmeasured definition.
- Pause (Sec 10) fires on the open "superintelligence precursor characteristics"
  list. The one measurable number (10^25 ops) gates the Sec 8 pause, not the Sec 9
  prohibition, and is self-keyed by the Secretary.

## Specimen 2 — UK Sobel bill, ControlAI-correlated (weak independence)
- Same shape as Specimen 1. ControlAI wrote the UK bill and consulted on the US
  one => correlated, not an independent confirmation.

## Specimen 3 — EU AI Act GPAI systemic risk (Art 51 + Annex XIII), more independent
- Multi-institutional legislative product (Commission proposal, Parliament +
  Council adoption), structurally distinct from a single-MP private bill.
  (No evidence of ControlAI's specific role; "more independent," not provably so.)
- Named referent "high-impact capabilities" (Art 3(64)) = "capabilities that match
  or exceed the capabilities recorded in the most advanced general-purpose AI
  models" — RELATIVE, MOVING-TARGET, unmeasurable in absolute terms.
- Measurable number demoted: Art 51(2) >10^25 FLOP => "shall be PRESUMED" to have
  high-impact capabilities (rebuttable presumption, not the classification).
  Art 51(3): Commission may amend the threshold via delegated act.
- Broader list is the parallel net: Art 51(1)(b) Commission designation "having
  regard to the criteria in Annex XIII" (params; modalities; benchmarks/autonomy/
  scalability/tools; >=10,000 registered business users; end-user count).
  Commission FAQ (digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu) states this pathway's purpose is
  "to capture models with an impact equivalent to the most advanced models" — the
  net that catches what the number misses.

Sources (primary): artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/51/ , /annex/13/ ,
digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu FAQ (GPAI models). sanders.senate.gov section-by-section.
