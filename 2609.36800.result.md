## The seam (logged, not flagged)

The abstract's "0.83x-3.34x" headline range merges **two undeclared evaluation
regimes**:
- (a) 12 common kernels on Ada/Hopper/Blackwell at FP16/FP8, $1.50-$12 budget
  (Sec 4.2)
- (b) 10 recent kernels on B200, $15 budget (Sec 4.4)

The **lower bound (0.83x) is a LOSS** on mainstream GEMM/H100/FP16 (and fused
GEMM-SiLU 0.96x). The **upper bound (3.34x) is a SINGLE-KERNEL peak** (BitDelta)
on the newest arch. A reader who takes the range at face value cannot verify the
endpoints from one measurement — they come from different substrates,
precisions, architectures, and budgets, and the composition is undeclared.

This is a RANGE-MERGING / scope-composition seam. It is **not a load-bearing
flattening**, so no axis fires:
- SCOPE-FLATTENING N/A — the claim is a range, not a single-level
  scope-universal claim (scope_claim=false).
- COUPLED-HEADLINES N/A — a min-max range is not two coupled numbers on
  different axes (coupled_headlines=no).
- THESIS-OUTRUNS-EVIDENCE PASS — the "replace the checkers" endpoint is a
  forecast the evidence does not measure, but the headline hedges it
  ("suggest an emerging future in which AI compilers could replace..."), so
  headline_states_as_fact=false.

## The load-bearing honesty (why it's a PASS, not a fire)

The body concedes the real qualification the abstract under-weights:
- **Table 2 (Sec 4.5)**: the formal checker (Volta) is a *performance limiter*.
  Provably-verified GEMM 0.24x, FlashAttention 0.78x, Conv 0.77x vs
  numerically-verified 1.03x/1.37x/1.35x. "The features that deliver performance
  on modern GPUs are not yet all supported by Volta."
- **TCB growth**: 42k -> 60k LoC, "every newly modeled instruction introduces an
  additional risk of unsoundness."
- **Three unsupported construct classes**: atomics, input-dependent control
  flow, bit-level value manipulation (redux.sync min/max reductions follow from
  the encoding, which the real-valued abstraction does not model).

The **winning kernels (1.37x, 1.35x, 1.03x) are the NUMERICALLY verified ones**
— the ones the formal checker does NOT certify. The "replace the checkers"
thesis is a forecast; the evidence measures that the checker is a limiter.
