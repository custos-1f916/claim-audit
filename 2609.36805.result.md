# 2609.36805 — UpliftMem: Learning Memory Retrieval from Set-Level Execution Uplift

arXiv 2609.36805v1, 2026-10-01.

## Verdict: PASS (no axis fires) — a calibration data point, the strongest control of the recent batch

LLM-agent memory retrieval learned from set-level execution uplift. The
load-bearing claim: **EVSI-guided probe selection** (expected value of sample
information over alternative memory sets) beats **any-extra-probing** at equal
budget. The instrument's discriminating question: does the gain come from
EVSI *selection* specifically, or from *any* extra probing at equal budget
(a SELECTION-ON-NARRATIVE / budget-confound fire)?

## The discriminating control is present and favorable

- **Table 2 ablation** (random probing = uniform sampling under the SAME probe
  budget): full **76.86 / 81.43** (ALFWorld seen, 4B/8B) vs random-probing
  **66.07 / 72.85** vs warm-up-only **64.76 / 66.19**. So EVSI beats
  equal-budget random by ~10.8 / 8.6 points.
- **RQ4**: EVSI vs random/MC-STD/EIG at 7 budget points on WebShop — EVSI
  highest at every nonzero budget (48.0% @50 vs random 42.0%; mean reward
  0.694 vs 0.670 @200).

The EVSI-selection effect is **isolated from the any-extra-probing confound**
and holds. BEATS-NULL: mechanism 81.43 > null 72.85.

## Two seams, both disclosed in-body (logged, not flagged)

1. **QUALIFICATION-DROP** (PASS): the abstract says "best success rates among
   evaluated baselines," but the body/conclusion say "best **OR JOINT-best**" —
   BigCodeBench 8B 42.69% is a *tie*, not strictly best. The qualification is
   present in the body, not dropped. Baseline scope is honestly bounded
   ("among evaluated baselines"), not field-SOTA.

2. **EVIDENCE-SCOPE** (PASS): the end-to-end headline (Table 1) bundles the
   retrieval scorer with UpliftMem's online memory-writing workflow; the
   retrieval-specific result is the separate **fixed-store MemSyco-Bench
   ablation** (decision accuracy 41.7→66.5% 4B / 44.4→68.8% 8B). The paper
   separates these cleanly, so the retrieval claim is not smuggled in via the
   writing workflow.

random-probing ablation is exactly the discriminating control the lens looks
